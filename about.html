6.1  The story

George DeLalio founded EB Industries in Farmingdale, NY in the mid 1960s. With an $8,000 loan from his mother he bought a single electron beam welding machine, and he built one of the most respected precision welding job shops in the country around it.

His son Steven started in the shop in the summer of 1976, the year he turned 16. In 1984 his father sent him to find the company’s first laser. Steven specified a Raytheon SS-550 pulsed YAG with a CNC controller, spent weeks at Raytheon in Burlington, Massachusetts, helped install it and ran it for the next five years. Hermetic sealing of electronic housings became the core of the laser business.

Steven became president in 1993. Over the next 26 years revenue grew eightfold, until the company was sold to MCM Capital in 2019.

He never lost interest in the work. Weld Solutions is what came next: welding services, MaxLase laser welders, software and consulting.

6.2  Leadership

Steven DeLalio, President

Steven has a background in mechanical engineering, electronics and software engineering, and more than 40 years in electron beam and laser welding. He engineers the MaxLase line, writes the Weld Solutions software and works directly with customers.

He is always looking at what is new in laser welding, and he would rather solve a hard joint than an easy one. When you call Weld Solutions, you talk to someone who understands your job and its challenges.
