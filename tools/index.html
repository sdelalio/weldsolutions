Install Weld Solver
Put this tool on your Home Screen and open it like an app.

On iPhone, Apple requires the final install tap from you:

  1. Tap Share in the browser.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. If you see Open as Web App, leave it turned on, then tap Add.
After that, the Weld Solver icon will appear on your Home Screen and this message will no longer appear when you launch the installed app.

Your phone can install Weld Solver directly.

Add Weld Solver to your Home Screen from your browser menu. Look for Install app or Add to Home Screen.
