Shop paperwork, from a photo.

Our ERP system turns photos of drawings, purchase orders and parts into the documents a job shop runs on. Snap a picture and the system builds the paperwork, in order, from RFQ to invoice. No retyping, no missing fields, and every document ties back to the same job.

It is simple on purpose. It does what a small or mid size shop actually needs and nothing it does not.
