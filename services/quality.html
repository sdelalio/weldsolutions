Quality

Quality you can trust.

Welding is a special process. You cannot inspect quality into a weld after the fact, so we control it before and during welding, and we keep the records to prove it.

Quality policy

Weld Solutions delivers welds that meet our customers’ requirements, the first time and on time.

We will not trade quality for delivery. When the two conflict, quality wins and the customer hears from us first.

We control every weld with qualified schedules, qualified operators and calibrated equipment, and we keep the records to prove it.

We act with integrity, protect our customers’ property and designs, comply with applicable requirements, and continually improve our quality management system.

Quality management system

Built to ISO 9001:2026.

WS-QM-001Quality Manual

Our documented quality system, with procedures, forms and records for every requirement.

WS-LWS-001Laser Welding Specification

Governs every laser weld we make. Your drawing, specification and purchase order come first. Available on request.

Contract review

Every order is reviewed before we accept it, so requirements and acceptance criteria are clear up front.

Weld schedules

Qualified before production and requalified when material, thickness, joint design or machine settings change beyond defined limits.

Operators

Trained and qualified for the work they do, with records on file.

Equipment

Measuring and inspection equipment calibrated on a defined schedule.

Traceability

Each part traces to its machine, weld parameters, operator and date.

Nonconformance

Documented nonconformance and corrective action. No repair or deviation without your written approval.

Certification

A Certificate of Conformance ships with every order.

Inspection and testing

We check what is inside the weld.

  • 100% visual inspection at 10X to WS-LWS-001
  • Metallographic cross sections to measure penetration, weld width, fusion profile and heat affected zone
  • First articles with cross sections when required
  • Dimensional inspection with calibrated equipment
