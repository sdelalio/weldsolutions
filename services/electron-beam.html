Why electron beam

Deep, narrow and clean.

The beam works in a vacuum, so you get deep, narrow welds with very little heat in the rest of the part.

  • Deep welds in thick sections, often in one pass
  • Clean welds on titanium, zirconium, niobium and other reactive metals
  • Low distortion and a small heat affected zone
  • Refractory and dissimilar metal joints
  • Vacuum sealed assemblies
