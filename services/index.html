1.1  Capabilities

What we do in the shop.

Laser welding

Pulsed and continuous wave fiber and Nd:YAG lasers, keyhole or conduction mode. Spot, seam, stitch and rotary welds on multi axis CNC stations with microscope and camera alignment.

Electron beam welding

Deep, clean welds in vacuum, engineered and performed by us, with AS9100 and ISO 13485 certified partners when a job needs a certification we do not offer yet. More on EB →

Weld development

Weld schedule development and qualification, first articles, and requalification limits set before production.

Fixturing

Fixtures built for fit up and repeatability, part after part.

Hard to join metals

Reflective, dissimilar and exotic metals. Filler wire or shims when the metallurgy does not cooperate.

Metlab

Cut, polish and etch cross sections in house to measure penetration, weld width, fusion profile and heat affected zone.
